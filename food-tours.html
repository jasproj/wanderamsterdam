Types of Tours

🧀 Cheese & Market Tour

Visit a kaaswinkel (cheese shop), taste aged Gouda, learn old vs. young, and hit Albert Cuyp Market for bitterballen and herring.

🍻 Craft Beer & Pub Crawl

Brouwerij 't IJ under the windmill, Proeflokaal Arendsnest (100+ Dutch beers), and hidden brown cafes in the Jordaan. 4-5 stops, 3 hours.

🥘 Multicultural Food Walk

De Pijp's Turkish bakeries, Surinamese takeaways, Indonesian toko shops, and a Moroccan mint tea stop. Amsterdam's real melting pot.

🍪 Stroopwafel & Sweets Workshop

Make your own stroopwafels in a bakery kitchen. Includes poffertjes, drop (Dutch liquorice), and oliebollen when in season.

Where to Go

Albert Cuyp Market

De Pijp

Amsterdam's biggest daily street market. 250+ stalls. Kibbeling (fried cod), Surinamese broodjes, fresh stroopwafels, and Dutch herring stands.

Jordaan

West Amsterdam

Brown cafes, artisan bakeries, and specialty cheese shops. Most Jordaan food tours start at Noordermarkt (Saturday organic market).

Foodhallen

Oud-West

Indoor food market in a former tram depot. 20+ vendors: Vietnamese bao, Dutch croquettes, oysters, craft cocktails. Rainy-day favourite.

Brouwerij 't IJ

East Amsterdam

Craft brewery under De Gooyer windmill. Tasting flights on the terrace. The most Instagrammed beer spot in the city.

Noordermarkt

Jordaan

Saturday farmers' market: organic bread, aged cheeses, smoked sausage, apple cake. Monday antique market same square.

Chinatown (Zeedijk)

Centrum

Dim sum, Cantonese roast duck, and the oldest Chinese temple in Europe. Walking distance from Centraal Station.

Tips

🐟 Try the herring

Raw herring with onions and pickles. Eat it the Dutch way — tilt your head back and lower it in.

🕙 Morning markets

Albert Cuyp opens at 09:00. The best stalls sell out by noon on weekends. Go early.

🥜 Allergy heads-up

Tell your guide about allergies before the tour starts. Most operators can substitute tastings.

💰 Budget 40-60 EUR

Tour prices usually include all tastings. Skip lunch — you'll eat enough for two meals.
