Types of Tours

🍺 Classic Pub Crawl

Group bar hop through Leidseplein and Rembrandtplein. Drink specials, skip-the-line at clubs, and a guide who knows every doorman. 4-5 stops.

🏚️ Brown Cafe Tour

The real Amsterdam drinking culture. Dark wood, sand floors, decades of tobacco stain on the ceiling. Jenever (Dutch gin) tastings in 300-year-old bars.

🍻 Craft Beer Tour

Brouwerij 't IJ, Oedipus, Two Chefs, and local taprooms. Guided tastings with brewery history. More hops, less chaos than a pub crawl.

🎶 Live Music & Bar Tour

Jazz bars in the Jordaan, blues on Leidseplein, and underground venues in Noord. The guide curates the lineup based on who's playing that night.

Where to Go

Leidseplein

Centrum

Amsterdam's main nightlife square. Clubs, comedy shows, and tourist bars. Loud, bright, crowded. The big pub crawls start here.

Rembrandtplein

Centrum

Second nightlife hub. Escape club, Hotel Arena, and late-night kebab joints. More club-focused than Leidseplein.

Jordaan

West Amsterdam

Quiet brown cafes, local regulars, and jenever tasting rooms. Cafe 't Smalle (1786) and Cafe Papeneiland (1642) are still pouring.

De Pijp

South Amsterdam

Wine bars, cocktail spots, and late-night Indonesian food. Younger, trendier crowd than Leidseplein.

Amsterdam-Noord

North of IJ

NDSM Wharf: repurposed shipyard with bars, festivals, and Oedipus brewery. Free ferry from Centraal runs until 3am.

Zeedijk

Centrum

One of Amsterdam's oldest streets. Dive bars, live music venues, and Chinatown restaurants open until 2am.

Tips

🆔 Bring your ID

Clubs check at the door. No ID, no entry — even if you look 40. Passport or EU ID card accepted.

💵 Cash for brown cafes

Older bars may not take cards under 10 EUR. Keep 20-30 EUR cash on you.

🚲 Don't bike drunk

It's illegal and dangerous on Amsterdam's narrow bridges. Night buses and taxis run until 4am.

🕙 Late start

Most pub crawls begin at 20:00-21:00. Nothing gets busy before 23:00. Dinner first.
