🚲 Amsterdam Bike Tours

Do as the Dutch do. Amsterdam has more bikes than people — and the best way to see the city is on two wheels.

Types of Tours

🏙️ City Bike Tour

Guided 3-hour ride through Vondelpark, Jordaan, Waterlooplein, and the canal ring. Flat, safe bike lanes the entire way. All fitness levels.

🌾 Countryside & Windmill Tour

Half-day ride north to Zaanse Schans or Waterland. Windmills, cheese farms, wooden houses, and polders. Ferry across the IJ included.

⚡ E-Bike Tour

Cover more ground with less effort. Popular for the countryside routes or full-day Amsterdam-to-Haarlem rides. Battery lasts 60+ km.

🔧 Bike Rental (Self-Guided)

Pick up a city bike or e-bike from shops near Centraal. Ride at your own pace. Most rentals include a lock, lights, and a map.

Where to Go

Vondelpark Loop

Zuid

3 km car-free loop through Amsterdam's biggest park. Families, joggers, and cafe terraces. Starting point for most city bike tours.

Amstel River Path

South Amsterdam

Follow the Amstel south past Magere Brug to Ouderkerk aan de Amstel. Flat, scenic, 20 km round trip. Pancake houses along the way.

Waterland Villages

North of Amsterdam

Ferry from Centraal to Amsterdam-Noord, then ride through Durgerdam, Ransdorp, and Holysloot. Wooden houses, sheep, and zero traffic.

Zaanse Schans

Zaandam

15 km ride from Amsterdam-Noord to the windmill village. Guided tours handle the ferry and route. Cheese and clog workshops on arrival.

Jordaan & Canal Ring

Centrum/West

Weave through the narrowest streets in the city. Hofjes, flower shops, and brown cafes. Best early morning before the crowds.

Haarlem via Kennemerduinen

West

Full-day ride through coastal dunes to Haarlem. Beach stop at Bloemendaal. Return by train (bikes allowed). 40 km.

Tips

🔔 Use your bell

Pedestrians wander into bike lanes constantly. One ring is polite. Two means move now.

🚦 Right has priority

At unmarked intersections, traffic from the right goes first. This catches every tourist off guard.

🔒 Always double-lock

Lock frame + wheel to a fixed object. Amsterdam loses 80,000 bikes a year to theft.

🌬️ Headwinds are real

The Netherlands is flat but windy. An e-bike on countryside routes saves your legs for the evening.
