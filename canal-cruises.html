Types of Tours

🚤 Open-Top Canal Cruise

The classic 60-75 minute loop through Herengracht, Keizersgracht, and Prinsengracht. Glass roof retracts in summer. Commentary in 20+ languages.

🥂 Evening & Dinner Cruise

Candlelit canal cruise with wine, cheese, or a full Dutch dinner. Popular for anniversaries and date nights. Departs at sunset.

🏠 Private Boat Charter

Rent a sloop or saloon boat with a skipper. Groups up to 12. Custom routes through Jordaan, Eastern Docklands, or Amstel.

🎫 Hop-On Hop-Off Boat

All-day pass with 10+ stops near Rijksmuseum, Anne Frank House, Centraal Station, and NEMO. Use it as transport between museums.

Where to Go

Herengracht

Canal Ring

The Gentlemen's Canal — grand 17th-century merchant houses, the Golden Bend, and Amsterdam's most photographed bridges.

Prinsengracht

Jordaan / Canal Ring

Passes Anne Frank House and Westerkerk. Wider than the inner canals, lined with houseboats and cafe terraces.

Amstel River

South Amsterdam

Opens up past Magere Brug (Skinny Bridge) into wider water. Evening cruises catch the bridge lights.

Eastern Docklands

East Amsterdam

Modern architecture, NEMO science museum, and the Maritime Museum from the water. Less crowded routes.

Jordaan Canals

Jordaan

Narrow side-canals too tight for big boats. Best explored by private sloop or kayak.

IJ Harbour

North Amsterdam

Open water behind Centraal Station. Views of A'DAM Tower, EYE Film Museum, and NDSM Wharf.

Tips

🌧️ Rain or shine

Most boats have glass roofs. Rain rarely cancels a cruise — just changes the vibe.

📸 Front seats fill first

Board 10 minutes early for an open-air bow seat. Best photos from the front.

🍺 BYO policy varies

Some boats allow outside drinks, others sell onboard. Check before you pack a picnic.

🕐 Skip the 2pm rush

Morning and evening cruises are quieter. Midday queues at Centraal can be 30+ minutes.
