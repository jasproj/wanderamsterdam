Types of Tours

🖼️ Rijksmuseum Guided Tour

Expert-led 2-hour tour of the Night Watch, Milkmaid, and the Gallery of Honour. Skip-the-line entry included. The essential Amsterdam museum.

🌻 Van Gogh Museum Tour

Chronological walk through 200+ paintings: Potato Eaters to Sunflowers to Almond Blossom. Timed-entry tickets sell out weeks ahead.

📖 Anne Frank House Tour

Timed entry only — no guided tours inside. Walking tours of the Jewish Quarter often include context before your visit. Book 6 weeks ahead.

🎨 Modern Art Tour

Stedelijk Museum (modern/contemporary), MOCO (Banksy, Kusama), and Foam (photography). Combined tickets available for museum-district trio.

Where to Go

Museumplein

Zuid

Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum, Stedelijk, and MOCO all within 500 metres. The cultural heart of Amsterdam.

Anne Frank House

Jordaan

Prinsengracht 263-267. The hiding place preserved as a museum. Tickets released online exactly 6 weeks in advance — set an alarm.

Rembrandt House

Jewish Quarter

Where Rembrandt lived and worked from 1639-1658. Original studio recreated. Etching demonstrations daily.

NEMO Science Museum

Eastern Docklands

Five floors of interactive exhibits. The rooftop terrace has the best free panoramic view in Amsterdam. Great for families.

Foam Photography Museum

Keizersgracht

Four exhibitions at a time in a canal house. Rotating shows from emerging and established photographers. Small, focused, excellent.

Tropenmuseum

Oost

Ethnographic museum in a grand colonial-era building. Collections from Southeast Asia, Africa, and Latin America. Under-visited gem.

Tips

🎫 Book weeks ahead

Van Gogh and Anne Frank House sell out. Rijksmuseum less so, but skip-the-line saves 45+ minutes in summer.

🕘 Go at opening

First hour is always quietest. By 11am, Museumplein is packed. Evening openings on Fridays at some museums.

💳 Museumkaart

Dutch museum pass: 65 EUR for a year, covers 400+ museums. Pays for itself in 2-3 visits.

📵 No flash photos

Photography allowed in most museums but flash damages paintings. Turn it off or use your phone camera.
