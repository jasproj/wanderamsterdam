Types of Tours

🏛️ History & Culture Walk

Dam Square to Begijnhof to the Jordaan. Covers the Golden Age, VOC trade, and how a swamp became a world capital. 2-3 hours.

🔴 Red Light District Tour

Evening walks through De Wallen with context: the history of tolerance, coffeeshop culture, and how the district actually works.

🎨 Art & Architecture Walk

From medieval timber houses to Rietveld and OMA. Covers Rijksmuseum exterior, Stedelijk, and hidden Art Deco gems in Zuid.

🆓 Free Walking Tour

Tip-based tours departing from Dam Square. 2.5 hours covering the highlights. Book online — groups fill quickly in summer.

Where to Go

Jordaan

West Amsterdam

Narrow streets, hofjes (hidden courtyards), independent galleries, and the Anne Frank House neighbourhood. The most walkable district.

De Wallen

Centrum

Oldest part of Amsterdam. Medieval architecture, Oude Kerk (1306), and the canals that started it all.

Jewish Quarter

East Centrum

Waterlooplein, Portuguese Synagogue, National Holocaust Memorial. Guided walks cover 400 years of history.

Museumplein

Zuid

Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum, Stedelijk, and Concertgebouw all within 500 metres. Architecture tours start here.

De Pijp

South Amsterdam

Albert Cuyp Market, Sarphatipark, and Amsterdam's most diverse food scene. Street art on nearly every block.

Vondelpark to Leidseplein

West/South

Green corridor through the city. Buskers, open-air theatre in summer, ending at Leidseplein's nightlife hub.

Tips

👟 Wear flat shoes

Cobblestones everywhere. Heels and smooth soles are a recipe for a twisted ankle.

☔ Layer up

Amsterdam weather changes hourly. A light rain jacket fits in any bag and saves the day.

💶 Tip your free guide

Free tours run on tips. Budget 10-15 EUR per person if the guide was good.

📱 Charge your phone

Guides move fast and stop often for photos. A dead battery at the Anne Frank House hurts.
